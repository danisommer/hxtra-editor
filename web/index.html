hxtra-editor: model catalog

The editor names models and parameters from a catalog extracted from your own HX Stomp firmware file. It is never shipped with the editor, since it holds Line 6 data. This page extracts it in your browser: the firmware is not uploaded anywhere.

starting...

Download hxs-catalog.json

Then

Put hxs-catalog.json next to hxtra-editor.exe, or load it in the editor's Catalog tab and press Install. Extract it again after changing firmware.

Supported: HX Stomp 3.80.0 stock, and HXtra builds of it. The extractor (hxtra-editor, GPL-3.0) runs on HXtra's firmware tools (GPL-3.0), commit ?. Not affiliated with Line 6 or Yamaha.
